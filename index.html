How to use each month
1. Update Report Settings with the new month and year.
2. In Extra Hours Staff, update or add staff who worked overtime — add each shift entry.
3. In Leave Staff, update dates, absent days and work % for anyone on leave.
4. In One-Off Replacements, add any single-shift cover staff.
5. Click Generate PDF to download the formatted report.
